🌌 Do You Know? – The 8 Planets

🌑

Mercury

About Mercury

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and has no atmosphere to trap heat. This causes extreme temperature changes, with scorching hot days and freezing nights. Its surface is rocky and heavily cratered.

🟡

Venus

About Venus

Venus is the hottest planet due to its thick carbon dioxide atmosphere. It rotates very slowly and in the opposite direction to most planets, and its surface is filled with volcanoes.

🌍

Earth

About Earth

Earth is the only known planet that supports life. It has liquid water, a breathable atmosphere, and a stable climate. About 71 percent of its surface is covered by water.

🔴

Mars

About Mars

Mars is called the Red Planet because of iron oxide on its surface. It shows evidence of ancient water and is studied to understand the possibility of past life.

🟠

Jupiter

About Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet and is made mainly of hydrogen and helium. It has a massive storm called the Great Red Spot and more than 90 moons.

🪐

Saturn

About Saturn

Saturn is famous for its rings made of ice and rock. It has a low density and many moons, including Titan, which may support complex chemistry.

🔵

Uranus

About Uranus

Uranus rotates on its side, causing extreme seasons. Its blue color comes from methane gas, and it has faint rings and many moons.

🌐

Neptune

About Neptune

Neptune is the farthest planet from the Sun and has the fastest winds in the solar system. It takes about 165 Earth years to complete one orbit.
